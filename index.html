I've written the upgraded MoodLens as one file, **moodlens-v2.html**, with all the HTML, CSS and JavaScript inside. I haven't opened it in a browser, so give it a quick try on your phone and tell me if anything looks off. It's free to use, needs no installs or accounts, and runs offline. ## What's new **More moods.** It now tells apart six moods instead of two: Joy, Love, Sadness, Anger, Fear and Surprise. If it isn't confident, it says "Unclear" instead of guessing. **Better results.** You get a big emoji badge, the mood name, a confidence label and a percentage bar for every mood. A row of word chips shows which words guided the decision. The whole card changes color to match the mood. **Mobile-friendly design.** - The layout is a single column that works on small screens and widens on tablets and desktops. - Buttons are at least 48px tall, so they're easy to tap. - The text box uses a 16px font, which stops iPhones from zooming in when you tap it. - Example sentences appear as tappable chips, so you can try it without typing. **Professional touches.** - A light/dark theme button, which starts from your phone's setting. - A character counter, and Ctrl/Cmd + Enter to analyze. - A "recent sentences" list you can tap to re-run. - Animated bars, which are switched off for people who prefer reduced motion. **Teaching that sticks.** If it gets a sentence wrong, tap the correct mood and it learns right away. Lessons are saved in your browser, and "Forget what I taught it" clears them. ## How it works, in short 1. **Moods** are defined in the `MOODS` object. Each has a name, emoji and color. 2. **Training data** is the `examples` list, with 8 sentences per mood. 3. **`tokenize()`** cleans a sentence into words and drops filler words like "the" and "am". 4. **`train()`** counts how often each word appears in each mood. 5. **`classify()`** is the AI itself, a Naive Bayes classifier. It scores all six moods and turns the scores into percentages that add up to 100%. 6. **`analyze()`** draws the result, and **`teach()`** adds your correction and retrains instantly. ## Add your own mood 1. Add a line to `MOODS`, for example `calm: { name: "Calm", emoji: "😌", color: "#3aa57a" }`. 2. Add about 8 example sentences for it in `examples`. The teach buttons and bars appear automatically. ## Limits - It is a small model with only 48 starting sentences, so it will make mistakes until you teach it more. - It doesn't understand negation, so "not happy" can still look like Joy. - It only understands English.
